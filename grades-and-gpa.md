# Grades and GPA

The undergraduate academic policy effective August 17, 2026 sets the grading scale and GPA rules in sections 3.10, 3.11, 3.15, and 3.20. [Undergraduate academic policy (PDF)](https://registrar.nu.edu.kz/sites/default/files/Academic%20Policies%20and%20Procedures%20for%20Undergraduate%20Programs%20of%20the%20autonomous%20organization%20of%20education%20Nazarbayev%20University%20as%20of%2005.03.2026.pdf)

## Grading scale

| Grade | Percentage | Quality points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 95-100 | 4.00 |
| A- | 90-94.9 | 3.67 |
| B+ | 85-89.9 | 3.33 |
| B | 80-84.9 | 3.00 |
| B- | 75-79.9 | 2.67 |
| C+ | 70-74.9 | 2.33 |
| C | 65-69.9 | 2.00 |
| C- | 60-64.9 | 1.67 |
| D+ | 55-59.9 | 1.33 |
| D | 50-54.9 | 1.00 |
| F | 0-49.9 | 0.00 |

A grades indicate performance above expectations across all learning outcomes. B grades indicate the student met all outcomes and exceeded some. C grades indicate satisfactory attainment of all outcomes, with C- marked as the threshold. D grades indicate partial attainment, and F indicates failure to achieve the outcomes.

In courses approved for pass/fail grading, a P is equivalent to C or higher.

## Semester GPA

Multiply each course’s ECTS by the quality points for its letter grade. Add the results and divide by all graded ECTS attempted for the semester, including failed courses. Exclude P grades and administrative notations such as withdrawals and audits.

The four courses below yield 47.36 weighted grade points. Dividing by 22 attempted ECTS gives a semester GPA of 2.15. The student earned 16 ECTS.

| Course | Grade | ECTS attempted | Weighted grade points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CHEM 101 General Chemistry I | B | 6 | 18.00 |
| CHEM 101L General Chemistry I Laboratory | A | 2 | 8.00 |
| WCS 150 Rhetoric and Composition | F | 6 | 0.00 |
| MATH 161 Calculus I | B- | 8 | 21.36 |

## Cumulative GPA

Divide the total grade points by the total graded ECTS attempted across the full period of study. Each course attempt counts toward its semester GPA. For a retaken course, only the last retake counts toward the final cumulative GPA and total earned credits.
