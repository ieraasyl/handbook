# Grades and GPA

## Grading scale

| Grade | Percentage | Quality points | Performance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 95-100 | 4 | Excellent, above the highest standards |
| A- | 90-94.9 | 3.67 | Excellent, meets the highest standards |
| B+ | 85-89.9 | 3.33 | Very good, meets high standards |
| B | 80-84.9 | 3.00 | Good, meets most standards |
| B- | 75-79.9 | 2.67 | More than adequate, demonstrates command of some material |
| C+ | 70-74.9 | 2.33 | Acceptable, meets basic standards |
| C | 65-69.9 | 2.00 | Acceptable, meets basic standards |
| C- | 60-64.9 | 1.67 | Acceptable, with several gaps |
| D+ | 55-59.9 | 1.33 | Minimally acceptable, with many gaps |
| D | 50-54.9 | 1.00 | Minimally acceptable, lowest passing grade |
| F | 0-49.9 | 0.00 | Failing |

## Semester GPA

Multiply each course’s ECTS by the quality points for its letter grade. Add the results and divide by all graded ECTS attempted, including failed courses. For example, the four courses below yield 47.36 weighted grade points. Dividing by 22 attempted ECTS gives a semester GPA of 2.15. The student earned 16 ECTS.

| Course | Grade | ECTS attempted | Weighted grade points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CHEM 101 General Chemistry I | B | 6 | 18.00 |
| CHEM 101L General Chemistry I Laboratory | A | 2 | 8.00 |
| WCS 150 Rhetoric and Composition | F | 6 | 0.00 |
| MATH 161 Calculus I | B- | 8 | 21.36 |

## Cumulative GPA

Divide the total grade points from graded courses taken in residence at NU by the total graded ECTS attempted in those courses. GPA tracks academic performance and can affect eligibility for honors or graduate school.
