# Academic Advising Office

## What the office helps with

The Academic Advising Office (AAO) supports first- and second-year undergraduates. Advisors help students choose and register for courses, understand university policies and degree requirements, plan their studies, review academic progress, and navigate transfers and probation. They can refer students to resources within or outside the university.

## Appointments

The 2024 intake handbook says incoming first-year students receive an advisor before the semester starts and must attend advising sessions to review their academic plans and progress, discuss degree requirements, and learn about campus resources.

1. In myRegistrar, open **Personal Schedule** and look under **Academic Info** for your assigned advisor.
2. Open your advisor’s calendar and book a 30-minute slot at least one hour in advance. [AAO appointments](https://aao.nu.edu.kz/appointment)
3. For an in-person meeting, use the room listed when you book. To meet online, let your advisor know after booking and they’ll send a Zoom link.

Third- and fourth-year students should contact their assigned faculty advisor. [Faculty advising](https://aao.nu.edu.kz/faculty_advisors)

## Student Support Fellowship

The Student Support Fellowship (SSF) offers tutoring and academic advice from junior and senior students. [AAO website](https://aao.nu.edu.kz)
