## How to read the reports

- Numeric grades are grades on the 0 to 4 grade-point scale. Reported means average individual numeric grades. Student GPA uses course credit weights. [GPA calculation](../grades-and-gpa.md#semester-gpa)
- The source groups regular letter grades under A, B, C, D, and F. Its detailed tables can also include P (pass), I (incomplete), AU (audit), and W or W/AW (withdrawal or administrative withdrawal). Percentages use the report’s letter and administrative grade count.
- SD is standard deviation. Blank cells preserve missing or suppressed values. The files retain the source subtotals, which can differ from sums of the rows shown in the report.
- See each original PDF for its methodology and any charts, including histograms and density plots.
