## Degree credits

Bachelor’s students on the four-year scholarship need at least 240 ECTS or the minimum set by their school. The degree programs incorporate the Undergraduate Core Curriculum Framework. Program requirements can change from year to year. Recommended schedules give progression grades that account for prerequisites. Transfer grades can differ by destination major. Engineering and Computing and Artificial Intelligence students may count courses from other schools as electives with school approval. See the [program index](../../../README.md#programs-by-school) for program-specific requirements.

## Electives

Core curriculum electives fall into Social Science, Humanities, or general electives. Some courses can count toward either Social Science or Humanities.

### Social Science

Courses with ANT, ECON, PLS, or SOC prefixes qualify.

### Humanities

HST courses other than HST 100, and courses with PHIL, REL, or WLL prefixes qualify.

### General electives

Courses from any school or major, including foreign language courses, can count if they fall outside the requirements of the intended major.

## Kazakh language

The courses required depend on placement at matriculation. The BA column applies to Anthropology, Economics, History, Political Science and International Relations, Sociology, and World Languages, Literatures and Cultures.

| Placement at entry | BA programs | Other majors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A2, Basic | KAZ 150, KAZ 201, KAZ 202, and one KAZ 3XX | KAZ 150, KAZ 201, and KAZ 202 |
| B1.2, Intermediate | KAZ 201, KAZ 202, and one KAZ 3XX | KAZ 201 and KAZ 202 |
| B2.2, Upper-intermediate | KAZ 202 and one KAZ 3XX or KAZ 4XX | KAZ 202 and one of KAZ 211, KAZ 212, or KAZ 3XX |
| C1.1, Advanced | Two KAZ 3XX or KAZ 4XX courses | Two KAZ 3XX or KAZ 4XX courses |
| No prior Kazakh | KFL 101, KFL 102, KFL 201, and KFL 202 | KFL 101, KFL 102, and KFL 201 |

- Graduates of Kazakh-language high schools are placed at Advanced and take two advanced KAZ courses.
- Students with Kazakh background who finished high school outside Kazakhstan should contact Aidar Balabekov or Uli Schamiloglu.
- The handbook lists KAZ 150 as a summer-only offering.
