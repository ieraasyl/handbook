## Major declaration

Declare after the first two semesters. By the end of spring, pass two of PLS 120, PLS 140, and PLS 150 with C or higher.

## Second-year transfer

Meet the same course and grade requirements and earn a CGPA of at least 2.5 by the end of spring.
