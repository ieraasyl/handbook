## Social Data Science minor

| Requirement | ECTS | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | 12 | Earn B or higher in MATH 275 and in one of PLS 211, ECON 211, MATH 321, or SOC 203. |
| Electives | 24 | Take at least two courses from two disciplines outside your major. See the eligible courses below. |
| Programming | 6 | Choose ECON 336, MATH 449, or a Computer Science programming course. |
| Total | 42 |  |

- PLS 417 Scientific Research in Political Science
- PLS 434 Text Analysis and Political Communication in the Information Age
- SOC 485 Statistics and Machine Learning for Social Reform

### Declaration

The participating departments are Economics, Mathematics, Political Science, and Sociology. The handbook lists two places per department, eight across those allocations, two additional places that can let one department take more than two students if another department has no interested applicants, and a maximum of ten declarations per year. Applications open at the end of year 3 fall and close at the end of year 4 fall.
