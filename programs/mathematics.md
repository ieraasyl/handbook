# BSc in Mathematics

## Major confirmation

Students enter their preferred science major. The university checks progression at the end of spring. Students who fall short can retake courses in summer, change major, or become undeclared. Passing the summer retakes confirms the major. Students who still fall short may change major, become undeclared, withdraw, or be dismissed.

Earn a GPA of at least 3.0 across MATH 161 and MATH 162 by the end of summer.

## Quantitative Finance minor (Mathematics track)

| Requirement | ECTS | Courses and grades |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introductory courses | 20 | MATH 161, ECON 101, and ECON 201. B- or higher in each. |
| Statistics or probability | 6 | MATH 321 Probability. B- or higher. |
| Economics electives | 12 | ECON 320 Money and Banking<br>ECON 326 The Economics of Financial Markets<br>ECON 446 Empirical Finance<br>ECON 4XX Corporate Finance<br>At least one 400-level course. |
| Mathematics electives | 18 | MATH 323 Actuarial Mathematics I<br>MATH 423 Actuarial Mathematics II<br>MATH 424 Financial Mathematics<br>MATH 425 Stochastic Processes<br>MATH 455 Stochastic Calculus |
| Total | 56 |  |

Mathematics majors must first complete ECON 101, MATH 161, ECON 201, and MATH 321 at B- or higher, plus one Mathematics elective from the list at B+ or higher.

Applications open at the end of the fall semester of year 3 and close at the end of the fall semester of year 4. The department selects up to 10 applicants. Complete all 56 ECTS by graduation. Earn B+ or higher in at least one ECON and one MATH elective at the 300 or 400 level. The other ECON and MATH electives require C- or higher. Elective lists are tentative.
