# BA in Sociology

## Major declaration

Declare after the first two semesters. Pass at least one SOC course with C or higher by the end of spring.

## Second-year transfer

By the end of spring, pass at least one SOC course with C+ or higher and earn a CGPA of at least 2.25. Students transferring from outside SSH need at least 2.5 CGPA and must be in good standing with respect to probation. The department may impose additional requirements.
