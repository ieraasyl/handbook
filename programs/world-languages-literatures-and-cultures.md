# BA in World Languages, Literatures and Cultures

**2024 intake handbook.** [Official handbooks by intake](https://aao.nu.edu.kz/handbook)

**Program total** 240 ECTS.

[Program index](../README.md#programs-by-school) | [Shared core requirements](../degree-and-core-requirements.md)

## Major declaration

Declare after the first two semesters. By the end of spring, earn B- or higher in a WLL or LING course. Foreign language courses are excluded.

## Second-year transfer

Pass at least one WLL or LING course with B- or higher.
