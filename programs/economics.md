## Major declaration and transfer

Declare after the first two semesters. By the end of spring, earn B- or higher in ECON 101, ECON 102, and MATH 161, and have a GPA of at least 2.75. The handbook allows one retake in ECON 101 and/or ECON 102.

Second-year transfers must meet the same course and grade thresholds by the end of spring and have a CGPA of at least 2.75. The handbook encourages taking intermediate courses in year 2.

## Quantitative Finance minor (Economics track)

| Requirement | ECTS | Courses and grades |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introductory courses | 20 | MATH 161, ECON 101, and ECON 201. B- or higher in each. |
| Statistics or probability | 6 | ECON 211 Economic Statistics. B- or higher. |
| Economics electives | 12 | ECON 320 Money and Banking<br>ECON 326 The Economics of Financial Markets<br>ECON 337 Empirical Finance<br>ECON 414 Advanced Monetary Policy<br>ECON 418 Corporate Finance and Governance<br>At least one 400-level course. |
| Mathematics electives | 18 | MATH 323 Actuarial Mathematics I<br>MATH 423 Actuarial Mathematics II<br>MATH 424 Mathematical Finance<br>MATH 425 Stochastic Processes<br>MATH 455 Stochastic Calculus |
| Total | 56 |  |

Before declaring, complete MATH 161, ECON 101, ECON 201, and ECON 211 at B- or higher, plus one Economics elective from the list at B+ or higher.

Applications open at the end of the fall semester of year 3 and close at the end of the fall semester of year 4. The department selects up to 10 applicants. Complete all 56 ECTS by graduation. Earn B+ or higher in at least one ECON and one MATH elective at the 300 or 400 level. The other ECON and MATH electives require C- or higher. Elective lists are tentative.
