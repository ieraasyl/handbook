## Electives

Choose six elective courses based on a primary and secondary area. The handbook says elective offerings may change with industry demand and faculty expertise.

### Materials and Manufacturing

- Structural Mechanics II
- Materials and Manufacturing II
- Additive Manufacturing
- Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems (MEMS) and Microsystems technology

### Thermofluids and Energy Applications

- Heating Ventilating & Air-Conditioning
- Fire Engineering
- Feasibility Analysis of Clean Energy Technologies
- Advanced Heat Transfer
- Transport Phenomena and Operations
- Multiphase Systems

### Design and Analysis

- Computer Aided Geometric Design
- Advanced Topics in Computational Fluid Dynamics
- Interdisciplinary Project - IDP
- MAE 353 Summer Internship

### System Dynamics and Control

- Fundamentals of Multi-Body Dynamics
- Advanced Control Systems and Industrial Automation
- MAE 466 Application of Optimization in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering

### Aerospace Engineering

- Vehicle Propulsion Systems
- Flight Mechanics
- Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Design
