### Summer internship

Students can use ELCE 399 for an external internship in year 3 summer. Find a faculty supervisor who is available throughout the term and a company that can issue an acceptance letter and completion certificate. The technical internship must exceed 200 hours and end by the last day of summer classes. The Career Advising Center can also help arrange an internship.
