# BA in History

**2024 intake handbook.** [Official handbooks by intake](https://aao.nu.edu.kz/handbook)

**Program total** 240 ECTS.

[Program index](../README.md#programs-by-school) | [Shared core requirements](../degree-and-core-requirements.md)

## Major declaration

Declare after the first two semesters. The handbook lists no additional declaration requirements.

## Second-year transfer

Starting after the fall semester of year 2, complete HST 100 with B- or higher.
