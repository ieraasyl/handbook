# BSc in Computer Science

**Program total** 240 ECTS.

## Transfer requirements

Complete CSCI 151 and CSCI 152. Applicants are expected to have a CGPA of at least 3.0, grades of B or higher in all programming and mathematics courses, and a record free of academic misconduct.

## Technical electives

Choose non-required courses at the 200 level or above from Computer Science or courses from this list.

- MATH 322 Mathematical Statistics
- MATH 351 Introduction to Numerical Methods with Applications
- MATH 407 Introduction to Graph Theory
- MATH 417 Cryptography
- PHYS 270 Computational Physics
- ROBT 205 Signals and Sensing with Lab
- ROBT 305 Embedded Systems
- ROBT 310 Image Processing
- ROBT 407 Statistical Methods and Machine Learning
- ROBT 414 Human-Robot Interaction

## Natural science electives

Natural science electives are courses at the 100 level or higher in Physics, Chemistry, Biology, or Geology. Courses designated for non-science majors are excluded.
